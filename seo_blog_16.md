# Men's Twill Trousers Style Guide — India 2026

A curated resource on the most versatile smart-casual trouser of 2026: **twill** — the
weave that bridges the gap between jeans and formal trousers.

## Why twill
- **Structured weave** — diagonal rib resists creasing, holds a clean line all day
- **Smart-casual middle ground** — more polished than linen, more relaxed than formal
- **One pair, two outfits** — office with a tucked shirt, weekend with a tee
- **Built for Indian weather** — durable and breathable enough for daily wear

## Quick rules
1. Relaxed-straight fit, clean break at the ankle
2. Buy colours in order: **Black → Navy → Sage Green**
3. Shirt tucked for office, tee untucked for weekend
4. One accessory finishes the look — watch or matched belt

## Colour priority
