# Men's Sage Green & Earthy-Tone Shirt Guide — India 2026

A curated resource on the defining men's shirt-colour trend of 2026: **sage green
and earthy tones** — muted shades that pair like neutrals.

## Why earthy tones
- **Look premium** — muted, considered, never loud
- **Suit most skin tones** — soft, grey-based shades flatter warm & cool tones
- **Pair like neutrals** — work with blue, black, charcoal & beige denim
- **Year-round** — fresh in summer, grounded in cooler months

## Quick rules
1. Choose muted sage over bright green
2. Pair earthy & pastel shirts with neutral denim
3. Buy in order: **Navy → Sage → Beige → a Pastel**
4. Keep shoes & accessories minimal — let the colour lead
