## Key principles
1. **Body type first** — the right cut depends on your build, not just waist size.
2. **Three fit checks** — waist holds without a belt, thigh pinch test, clean ankle break.
3. **Relaxed fit = lowest risk** — `relaxed-fit jeans for men` suit the widest range of builds.
